Statecraft
Pakistan fuel prices (effective 25 Nov)
Under an OGRA notification effective from 25 November, petrol is priced at Rs 318 per litre (down Re 1) and high-speed diesel at Rs 353 per litre (down Rs 4).
Pakistan sugar sector deregulated
The federal cabinet approved a sugar-sector deregulation policy. Export subsidies end; sugar moves freely between provinces; cane prices are left to the market; new mills face no restriction. All provinces agreed to the arrangement.
Security
VMware advisory VMSA-2026-0011 — 23 vulnerabilities
Broadcom's advisory VMSA-2026-0011 patches 23 vulnerabilities across VMware products. The headline flaw is CVE-2026-123903, an authentication bypass in vCenter rated CVSS 9.8. vCenter 8.0 is fixed in release 8.0 U3m. No active exploitation has been reported at time of publication.
Crypto
South Korea FSC draft digital-asset act
The Financial Services Commission released a draft digital-asset act that caps a major shareholder's stake in a crypto exchange at 20%, or 34% with regulatory approval. The act initially limits won-denominated stablecoin issuance to bank-majority consortia. The draft now proceeds to the National Assembly for review.
AI
Shirudo — OpenAI and Cisco open-source eBPF exploit shields
OpenAI and Cisco jointly open-sourced Shirudo, an eBPF-based security layer designed to block exploitation of kernel vulnerabilities at runtime. The tool operates at the system-call level, intercepting attack patterns before they reach vulnerable code paths.
Hong Kong — October visitor arrivals up 12%
Hong Kong recorded 5.13 million visitor arrivals in October 2026, a year-on-year increase of 12%, signalling continued recovery in inbound tourism.
