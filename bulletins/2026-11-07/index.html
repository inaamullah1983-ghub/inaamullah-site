Reward Heaven Text Bulletin · 07 Nov

Statecraft
Pakistan: roadmap ordered for 100% digital payments
The Prime Minister asked for time-bound targets toward fully digital payments. Most federal and provincial institutions are to go digital by December 2026, and Asaan Assignment Account cheques end on 15 November, replaced by Raast.
Security
SonicWall SSL-VPN authentication bypass exploited
CVE-2026-19868, a SAML authentication bypass in SonicOS SSL-VPN, is exploited in attacks. CISA added it to its KEV catalogue on 6 November with a 20 November deadline for US federal agencies. There is no workaround; patch to the fixed SonicOS release listed in SonicWall's advisory.
Crypto
Nigeria licenses stablecoins and bans algorithmic ones
From 5 November, Nigeria's central bank requires licences and per-token approval for stablecoins, bans algorithmic stablecoins, and gives existing operators 180 days to apply.
TikTok Shop gets a stand-alone app
A separate TikTok Shop app is live in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and the Philippines on iOS and Android. TikTok has not commented.
AI
METR audit: Anthropic's AI R&D pace roughly doubled
An independent METR assessment puts capability growth at about 1.9x the historical rate (95% range 1.5x to 2.8x) and attributes about 80% of it to AI. Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative.
Code
Cursor cloud agents now build, host and publish web apps
From a prompt, Cursor's cloud agents produce a hosted web app with an optional Postgres database and Cursor SSO sign-in. Security checks run first, and nothing goes live without an explicit publish.
Computing
Google's $17B Wyoming data centre leans on fuel cells
Google plans its first Wyoming data centre, a $17B project partly powered by a planned 702 MW fuel-cell plant with carbon capture, amid growing local resistance to data centres.
Travel
Airbnb's AI travel agent due in the first half of 2027
Airbnb's Q3 letter says an AI travel agent will launch in H1 2027, building maps, comparisons and itineraries and handling rebookings and refunds.
