Pakistan's October remittances reach $3.9bn
Overseas Pakistanis sent home $3.9bn in October, up 13.8% year on year. July to October inflows total $14.8bn, up 14%.

Microsoft's November fixes include an exploited Outlook zero-day
The November update fixes over 1,000 CVEs, including CVE-2026-124194, an exploited Outlook spoofing flaw. CISA set a 24 November federal deadline.
AI agents formally verify Signal's protocol code
Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code generated over six million lines of Lean proof in four weeks, verifying core security guarantees of Signal's protocol code. The proof assumes perfect cryptography.

NYSE chose Avalanche technology to record and settle trades on its planned tokenized venue. Regulatory approval is still pending and no launch date has been set.
