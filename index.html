About the Publisher

Inaam Ullah is a former State Service Officer (CSS Scholar), Vice President of the Chess Federation of Pakistan, and Systems Architect with hands-on experience across federal, provincial, and local administration in Pakistan.

He publishes Reward Heaven — a daily source-verified bulletin covering AI, code, chess, statecraft, crypto, data, security, and travel. Every figure is checked against two independent sources before publication. Video narration is AI-generated; corrections are posted in comments.
